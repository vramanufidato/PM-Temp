# 🚀 Project Phoenix: A Hybrid PMP/Agile Framework for Life Transition

### Managing Health Recovery, Caregiving, and Career Pivot under Resource Constraints

## 📌 Executive Summary

**Project Phoenix** is a structured, professional-grade framework designed to manage a complex life transition following a career gap due to clinical depression. By treating recovery and upskilling as a formal project, I applied **PMI standards** and **Agile methodologies** to ensure progress while maintaining high-quality care for a family member.

This repository serves as a portfolio of my **Project Management skillset**, demonstrating resilience, systems thinking, and the ability to maintain governance in high-volatility environments.

---

## 🏗 The Framework: Hybrid Methodology

I utilized a **Hybrid Model** to balance the need for long-term predictability with short-term adaptability.

### 🔹 Waterfall (The Foundation)

Used for high-level governance and "Initial State to End State" planning.

* **Project Charter:** Defining the "Definition of Done" for my career pivot.
* **WBS:** Breaking down 100+ tasks into 3 distinct workstreams (Health, Care, Upskilling).
* **Communication Matrix:** Managing stakeholder expectations (Medical team, family, mentors).

### 🔹 Agile/Scrum (The Execution)

Used for the daily/weekly grind where energy levels (Resources) were unpredictable.

* **2-Week Sprints:** Planning "Velocity" based on a bi-weekly health assessment.
* **Daily Standups:** Personal accountability checks on blockers and mood.
* **Sprint Retrospectives:** Data-driven adjustments to the learning path.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* **Upskilling Velocity:** Targeted completion of [Insert Skill/Cert] within 4 Sprints.
* **Health Stability:** Maintenance of a "Green" status (consistent sleep/therapy) for 3 consecutive Sprints.
* **Caregiving Reliability:** Zero missed medical appointments or critical care duties.
